# Resume PDF Rendering Specification

**Status:** APPROVED VISUAL BASELINE  
**Approved:** 2026-09-25  
**Reference implementation:** Mor Furniture AI Solutions Manager final PDF

## Purpose

This specification defines the visual and behavioral contract for opportunity-specific resume PDFs generated by JobsearchProblems.

The approved design is now frozen. Future job-specific resumes should change content, not redesign the page.

## Architecture

Resume generation must remain separated into distinct concerns:

1. Candidate Knowledge and completed Evaluation provide validated evidence.
2. Resume composition selects and writes opportunity-specific content.
3. Structured resume content is passed to the renderer.
4. The renderer applies this approved visual system.
5. The rendered PDF is visually and ATS checked.
6. Candidate review occurs before approval/submission.

The renderer must not invent, rewrite, or strengthen unsupported candidate claims.

## Approved Visual System

- US Letter page.
- Clean one-page layout when strong relevant evidence fits naturally.
- Two-column page-one design.
- Pale blue left sidebar.
- White main experience column.
- Navy section headings and accents.
- Thin horizontal section rules.
- Large centered candidate name.
- Centered target positioning line.
- Compact centered contact line.
- No candidate photo.
- Main column receives visual priority over sidebar.
- Consistent left edge for all main-column headings, role rows, bullets, and Education.
- Employment dates are right aligned and must not collide with role/company text.
- Sidebar background must visually reach the bottom of the page.
- Job title to first bullet spacing should be compact but clearly separated.
- Bullet-to-bullet and job-to-job spacing must preserve scanability.
- Do not compress the entire page merely to force content onto one page.

## Sidebar

The sidebar is intended for high-value supporting information, not narrative career history.

Permitted sections include:

- Core Skills
- Tools & Platforms
- Certifications
- Selected Wins

Sidebar content may be tailored to the Opportunity, but all claims must be supported by Candidate Knowledge.

The sidebar must not expand into the main column.

## Main Column

Preferred order:

1. Professional Summary
2. Professional Experience
3. Education

The summary is opportunity-specific.

Professional Experience should prioritize the strongest and most relevant confirmed evidence. Older or lower-priority evidence may be shortened when needed, but factual history must remain accurate.

Education currently renders as:

**B.A., Criminal Justice & Corrections | Sam Houston State University**

## Content Behavior

The visual template is fixed. Content adapts to the Opportunity.

When content is slightly too long:

1. preserve required/highest-relevance evidence;
2. shorten redundant wording;
3. remove the lowest-priority optional bullet or supporting detail;
4. preserve critical ATS terminology when it is truthfully supported;
5. never reduce readability below the approved baseline merely to force one page.

A second page is preferable to unreadable text, clipping, unsupported compression, or removal of material evidence.

## Evidence Rules

Resume claims must come from validated Candidate Knowledge.

Preferred evidence state:

- `confirmed`
- current/non-superseded
- traceable to Work Experience, Project, Evidence Story, Skill, or explicit Tool relationship

Do not use `candidate_review_needed` evidence as confirmed proof.

Tool taxonomy membership by itself is not proof of proficiency.

Opportunity-specific wording may translate a confirmed accomplishment into employer-relevant language, but must not materially change the underlying fact.

## PDF QA

Every final PDF must pass:

### Visual QA

- correct page count;
- no clipping;
- no overlapping text;
- no content crossing column boundaries;
- sidebar reaches the page bottom;
- role/company rows align consistently;
- dates align consistently;
- title-to-first-bullet spacing is consistent;
- section spacing remains intentional;
- Education remains aligned with main content;
- no accidental blank/near-empty page.

### ATS / Parse-Back QA

Extract text from the rendered PDF and verify:

- candidate name is recoverable;
- contact information is recoverable;
- section headings are recoverable;
- role/company/date information is recoverable;
- material keywords/skills intentionally included are recoverable;
- critical quantified outcomes are recoverable;
- Education is recoverable;
- reading order remains understandable.

## Versioning

The approved visual baseline is versioned separately from resume content.

A generated Application Material should ultimately preserve:

- Opportunity / JD snapshot
- Evaluation version
- Candidate Knowledge evidence snapshots
- resume-content version
- renderer/template version
- rendered PDF artifact
- candidate approval/submission state

Once a resume is approved or submitted, that exact artifact must remain reproducible and must not silently change when Candidate Knowledge later changes.
