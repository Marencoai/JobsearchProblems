# Resume PDF Rendering Specification

**Status:** APPROVED CANONICAL BASELINE  
**Approved:** 2026-09-28  
**Canonical renderer:** `executive-brief-two-page-v1`

## Purpose

This specification defines the stable visual and content-budget contract for opportunity-specific resumes generated by JobsearchProblems.

The visual architecture is frozen. Future resumes should tailor evidence and wording to the Opportunity without redesigning the document.

## Core Story

The two pages have different jobs:

**Page 1: This is the value Diana can bring.**

**Page 2: This is the career evidence that proves it.**

A hiring manager should be able to understand the candidacy from Page 1 alone, then use Page 2 to validate the claims against chronological experience.

## Page 1: Executive Brief

Fixed order:

1. Candidate header
2. Professional Summary
3. Core Capabilities
4. Selected Impact
5. Selected Projects & Systems
6. Supporting Information

### Professional Summary
- opportunity-specific;
- roughly 60-90 words;
- explains the candidate's value for this role;
- does not repeat the full work history.

### Core Capabilities
- maximum 12;
- chosen from confirmed Candidate Knowledge;
- tailored to the Opportunity;
- rendered in a compact three-column grid.

### Selected Impact
- exactly 4 when sufficient confirmed evidence exists;
- metrics should represent the strongest outcomes relevant to the Opportunity;
- do not duplicate a metric solely to fill space.

### Selected Projects & Systems
- maximum 3;
- may include systems, implementations, transformations, or projects;
- selected for relevance to the employer's actual problem;
- each item must remain evidence-backed.

### Supporting Information
- Tools & Platforms on the left;
- Certification & Education on the right;
- tools should generally remain at 12 or fewer;
- tool taxonomy membership alone is not evidence of proficiency.

## Page 2: Professional Experience

Page 2 contains the complete selected chronological work history with no Page 1 experience section.

Each role may include:
- title;
- employer;
- dates;
- one concise scope line;
- evidence-backed bullets.

Content budgets:
- recent / high-relevance roles: up to 3-4 bullets;
- mid-priority roles: up to 2-3 bullets;
- older / supporting roles: 1-2 bullets.

The content engine should shorten or remove lower-priority bullets before reducing readability or creating a third page.

## Visual System

- US Letter;
- two pages by design;
- single-column reading order;
- navy headings and restrained blue accents;
- thin section rules;
- pale blue / gray supporting blocks;
- no photo;
- large centered Page 1 name and opportunity-specific headline;
- compact continuation header on Page 2;
- right-aligned dates;
- readable fixed typography;
- no full-height sidebar;
- intentional white space rather than compressed text;
- Page 1 is visually structured like an executive brief;
- Page 2 reads like a clean chronological proof record.

## Layout Rules

The renderer should never solve overflow by shrinking all text.

Priority order:
1. preserve readability;
2. preserve the fixed two-page architecture;
3. preserve the strongest evidence;
4. remove redundant or low-priority content;
5. shorten wording without changing meaning.

If the selected content cannot fit in two pages while preserving readability, resume composition must be revised before rendering.

## Evidence Rules

Every resume claim must come from validated Candidate Knowledge.

Preferred evidence state:
- `confirmed`;
- current / non-superseded;
- traceable to Work Experience, Project, Evidence Story, Skill, or supported Tool relationship.

Do not use `candidate_review_needed` evidence as confirmed proof.

Opportunity-specific wording may translate confirmed accomplishments into employer-relevant language but may not materially strengthen or invent the underlying fact.

## QA

Every final PDF must pass both visual QA and ATS / parse-back QA.

### Visual QA
- exactly 2 pages;
- no clipping;
- no overlap;
- no accidental third page;
- no near-empty page;
- no role/date collisions;
- consistent spacing;
- Page 1 sections remain in the approved order;
- all selected Professional Experience remains on Page 2;
- supporting blocks render cleanly;
- typography remains at approved readable sizing.

### ATS / Parse-Back QA
Verify extraction of:
- candidate name;
- contact information;
- section headings;
- capabilities;
- impact metrics;
- project names and descriptions;
- role / employer / dates;
- quantified outcomes;
- tools;
- certification;
- education.

Reading order must remain understandable.

## Stress-Test Approval

The canonical architecture was approved after rendering against two materially different Opportunities:

- Mor Furniture For Less Inc - AI Solutions Manager
- SoundHound AI - Account Manager, Enterprise

The two test resumes used the same visual architecture while presenting different:
- headlines;
- summaries;
- capabilities;
- impact metrics;
- projects;
- tools;
- role emphasis.

Both rendered as exactly two pages and passed visual and ATS text-extraction review.

## Legacy Renderers

`modern-sidebar-v1` is retained only for historical reproducibility of prior Application Materials.

`professional-two-page-v1` is a superseded prototype and should not be selected by new active Application Templates.

Do not silently alter historical artifacts that were produced with those renderer keys.

## Versioning

A generated resume should preserve:
- Opportunity / JD snapshot;
- Evaluation version;
- Candidate Knowledge evidence snapshots;
- resume-content version;
- renderer key/version;
- rendered PDF artifact;
- candidate approval/submission state.

Once approved or submitted, the exact material version and rendered artifact remain historical and must not silently change.
